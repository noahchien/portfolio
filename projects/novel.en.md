# Long-form fiction & content tooling

[繁體中文](novel.md)　／　**English**

Long stories create a practical challenge: chapters change, characters learn things at different times, and worldbuilding keeps growing. I want to spend more time on the story without losing track of what has already been decided.

## The workflow

- Git keeps revisions traceable and separates finalized work, drafts, and historical versions.
- JavaScript tools check chapter numbers, titles, and source consistency, then generate reading copies.
- Structured character and relationship data supports a zoomable relationship map.
- Mobile review pages group up to 50 outlined chapters per page and label integrated work separately from review candidates.
- I make creative decisions and review changes; AI assists with drafting, tooling, and organization.

## Where the project stands

The main novel has finalized manuscript through Chapter 35, with Chapters 36–37 still being revised. Its longer-term plan extends to Chapter 3500. Mobile review copies cover Chapters 38–3500: 3463 outlined chapters, including 1463 from integrated material and 2000 review candidates. These are planning and management figures, not 3500 finished manuscript chapters.

## My role

Story direction, character decisions, requirements, revision feedback, and review. AI helps with drafting, implementation, and organization; version and progress records support continuity between work sessions.

The private reader is hosted independently of the working computer. Mac remains the primary Git repository, with GitHub used as a private backup.

This public case explains the workflow and design. Unpublished manuscripts, complete outlines, and private review access details remain private.
