# Engineering experience | Understand the problem, connect the work

[繁體中文](work.md)　／　**English**

I've worked across securities, banking, insurance, and research environments. The technologies and business needs differ, but each project needs clear requirements, implementation, communication, and validation to move toward delivery.

Here are a few examples of what I was responsible for and the problems the work aimed to solve.

## Securities risk control and trading workflows

**The problem:** Ongoing development and maintenance require reliable ways to reproduce issues and validate changes in trading and risk-control systems.

**My responsibilities:** At Concord Securities, I worked on development coordination and maintenance, reconstructing test scenarios from transaction records and logs to support troubleshooting. I also led the move from manual convertible-bond operations to electronic trading workflows, using technologies including C/C++ and Node.js.

**The change:** More convenient testing and issue investigation, with less manual operational work. To me, these improvements help reserve people's attention for the parts that need their judgment.

## Insurance payment platform

At Fubon Life, I contributed to a maturity-benefit payment platform through architecture, database design, code reviews, and development. I also coordinated vendor progress, version control, and requirements discussions. The project used Java 8, Spring/Spring Boot, REST APIs, and Ext JS, deployed on Linux/JBoss.

## Trust agency system upgrade

At CTBC Bank, I maintained a trust agency service system and worked on architecture, development, and testing for its end-of-support upgrade. I also supported trust stock-affairs requirements, with attention to existing functionality and change management.

## Development tools and data platforms

At the Institute for Information Industry, I worked on Java/Spring and JavaScript/AngularJS development, Redmine administration, Python data-observation services, and DevOps activities using Git, Jenkins, and Docker. My experience also includes Elasticsearch search and bot applications.

## What I'd like to bring to the next team

Development experience helps me look closely at code and architecture. Analysis and project coordination remind me to look outward at requirements and people. I want to keep connecting those perspectives to solve practical problems with a team.

These summaries are based on my resume. No unverified improvement percentages are used. Company systems, customer data, transaction records, and credentials remain private; any future public demonstrations will use separate simulated data.
