# Engineering experience

[繁體中文](work.md)　／　**English**

I've built systems for securities, banking, insurance, and research organizations. Here is what I was responsible for in a few of those roles.

## Securities risk control and trading workflows

**The problem:** Ongoing development and maintenance require reliable ways to reproduce issues and validate changes in trading and risk-control systems.

**My responsibilities:** At Concords Securities, I worked on development coordination and maintenance, reconstructing test scenarios from transaction records and logs to support troubleshooting. I also led the move from manual convertible-bond operations to electronic trading workflows, using technologies including C/C++ and Node.js.

**Result:** Faster testing and investigation, and less manual work.

## Insurance payment platform

At Fubon Life, I contributed to a maturity-benefit payment platform through architecture, database design, code reviews, and development. I also coordinated vendor progress, version control, and requirements discussions. The project used Java 8, Spring/Spring Boot, REST APIs, and Ext JS, deployed on Linux/JBoss.

## Trust agency system upgrade

At CTBC Bank, I maintained a trust agency service system and worked on architecture, development, and testing for its end-of-support upgrade. I also supported trust stock-affairs requirements, with attention to existing functionality and change management.

## Development tools and data platforms

At the Institute for Information Industry, I worked on Java/Spring and JavaScript/AngularJS development, Redmine administration, Python data-observation services, and DevOps activities using Git, Jenkins, and Docker. My experience also includes Elasticsearch search and bot applications.

Company systems, customer data, and transaction records are not published.
